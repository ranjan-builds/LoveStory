Chapter 01

The Beginning of My Questions

It started after 2025. When I first proposed to you, you said no. I accepted that and asked you to block me because I didn't want to drag myself through unreciprocated feelings. But you didn't block me.

Instead, after some time passed, you started reaching out. Even when I was emotionally distant, you called and texted me saying:

"Tu mere se baat nai karta."
"Tu meko bhav nai deta."

I wondered why you were saying these things if you had rejected me. I thought maybe you wanted me to stay invested. Gradually, my attraction grew again, but I warned myself: "Can I leave?" because I knew if things got deeper, I would be hurt.

Chapter 02

Sending the PDF document: Laying down all my unanswered questions, boundary breaches, and hurt without anger.

My Story — What I Felt & What Happened.pdf

Delivered on 17 August 2026

The Core Question

"I chose you among many other options. I closed my heart to everyone else because I genuinely cared for you. But your pattern—disappearing without a word, replying hours late when free, making time for movies and friends while leaving me on read—made me feel like a convenient option."

On 17 August, after observing a repeating pattern of ghosting—from the Puri trip to the month-long silences, helping with your thesis despite my laptop breaking, and receiving mixed signals—I reached my threshold. I didn't want to insult myself by begging for communication.

From Page 5-6 of the Document:
"You told me: 'Tu mere liye tadpata rahega but mere paas tere liye time nai hoga.' That answer explained everything to me. You were telling me that you wouldn't make time for me. I told you 'One-sided love is painful but beautiful,' and you agreed. It felt like I was trapped in a one-sided contract."
The 17 August Chat Moment:
"Gradually I'm losing trust and interest from you... If you don't have enough time, then it is better not to chat with me. Chat only if you have time."

"If you love someone else, tell me. I will step back respectfully and never stand between you two. I just wanted the truth, even if it hurt."

Chapter 03

Her Apology & The Three Days of Hope

After reading the PDF document on 17 August, something shifted. She acknowledged that she had genuinely done wrong by me and apologized for the pain her inconsistent communication had caused.

She promised that she would prove her love through her actions. For the next three days, I experienced the warmth and consistent communication I had been longing for.

Those three days felt like a sudden ray of light after months of fog. It proved to me that my hopes weren't foolish, and that effort was possible.

A Moment of Relief

Her apology gave me hope. I wanted to believe that our bond was capable of healing, and that we could rebuild trust through mutual care.

On 20 August, her menstrual period began, and she became less available once again. I understood that physical discomfort and emotional exhaustion during this time naturally take a toll on anyone.

Empathetic Care

I deeply cared for her health and comfort. I never expected her to strain herself or ignore her physical well-being.

The Need for Clarity

At the same time, I hoped for small reassuring updates so I wouldn't mistake health-related silence for a return of the old ghosting pattern.

Chapter 07

Growing Closer & The Unspoken Curiosity

After meeting on 9 September, our emotional investment deepened. Our conversations became far more personal, touching on intimate aspects of life and future thoughts.

She shared vulnerable details about her past with me. I deeply appreciated her trust and willingness to open her heart. It made me feel valued as a safe space for her.

Yet, I noticed a quiet asymmetry: while she shared her past, she rarely asked about my past or showed curiosity about my personal life experiences.

I do not claim to know her reasons. Perhaps she assumes she already knows me, or perhaps her mind is preoccupied. I simply long for mutual curiosity—a bond where both people hold equal desire to know each other's inner worlds.

A healthy relationship is built when two people listen with equal wonder and ask about each other's dreams.
